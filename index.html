Essence & Conjure

Essence is a language for specifying combinatorial problems. This site brings together tools, learning materials, demos, and research from the Essence and Conjure community.

Notable tools

  • Conjure — Translates Essence specifications into constraint models.
  • Savile Row — Reformulates constraint models and translates them for a range of solvers.
  • Conjure Oxide — A new constraint modelling tool, currently in development.

Playground and demos

  • Conjure playground — Write and run Essence in your browser using Conjure WebAssembly. Coming soon.
